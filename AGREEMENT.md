AI USE AND APPROVAL AGREEMENT
Arisu Office

You must read and accept this before using Arisu Office. If the text changes, you will be asked to accept it again.

1. WHAT ARISU OFFICE DOES
Arisu Office uses AI "employees" to help run a Facebook content service for a business: post ideas, post drafts, a quality review of the drafts, draft replies to public comments, monthly content plans, visual and Reel briefs, summaries of pasted notes or competitor posts, a proposed brand profile for a new client, follow-up and payment-reminder wording, and a summary of how recent Page posts performed. The software also keeps notes, runs searches, tracks invoices, and shows a daily briefing. The AI prepares work. People decide.

2. WHAT THE AI MAY DO ON ITS OWN
- Suggest ideas, plans, visual briefs, and Reel scripts.
- Write draft posts, draft replies, draft follow-ups, and draft reminder wording.
- Review drafts and flag problems.
- Summarise text a person pastes in, and summarise recent Page post counts.
- Propose a brand profile. A person must apply it.
Everything the AI produces is only a suggestion. It is saved inside Arisu Office and is not visible on Facebook or to any customer.

3. WHAT THE AI MAY NEVER DO WITHOUT A PERSON'S APPROVAL
- Publish or schedule a post on a Facebook Page.
- Send or post any reply, message, follow-up, or reminder. In this version of Arisu Office the system never sends replies, follow-ups, reminders, or invoices at all. A person copies the text and sends it.
- Contact a customer or any other person.
- Spend money, run ads, or change a budget.
- Delete content, or change Page settings or roles.
- State a price, discount, date, guarantee, or testimonial that a person did not provide.
- Change a client's saved brand profile.

4. WHAT THE AI MUST NEVER DO, EVEN WITH APPROVAL
- Pretend to be a human, or pretend a post was written by someone it was not.
- Handle payments or bank details.
- Calculate, change, confirm, or mark paid any invoice or amount. All amounts are calculated by the software from numbers a person typed in. Only a person on the team can confirm that money was received.
- Give medical, legal, tax, or financial advice or guarantees.
- Use one client's information for another client.
- Post engagement bait, or content that breaks Facebook's rules or the law.

5. HOW APPROVAL WORKS
1) The AI writes a draft. 2) Automatic checks and an AI reviewer flag problems. 3) A person reads the exact text and approves or rejects it. 4) Only an approved post can be published. 5) If anyone edits the text after approval, the approval is cancelled and the post must be approved again. Every approval is recorded with the person's name and the time. The approved text is the only text that can be published.
A client may require that only the client's own account can approve their posts.

6. INVOICES AND PAYMENTS
- Invoices are billing statements prepared by the operator. They are not a substitute for any receipt or invoice that tax law requires; the operator should ask an accountant what applies to them.
- A sent invoice cannot be edited. A mistake is fixed by voiding it and issuing a new one.
- A client may report that they paid and give a reference. That is only a report. The invoice becomes paid only when a person on the team confirms the money was received.
- Arisu Office does not collect or hold money.

7. YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES
- AI makes mistakes. It can be wrong, out of date, or tone-deaf. Read every draft before you approve or send it.
- By approving or sending, you take responsibility for it.
- Give accurate information about the business. Do not put passwords, ID numbers, health information, or other private personal data into topics, notes, pasted text, or brand profiles.
- Only connect Facebook Pages that you are allowed to manage, and only post content you have the right to use.
- Notes marked as pinned are sent to the AI as background for that business. Do not pin anything private.

8. YOUR DATA
- What is sent to the AI provider (OpenAI) to do the work: the brand profile, pinned notes, the topic you type, text you paste in, the wording facts for a reminder (invoice number, amount, due date, client name), and recent Page post text and counts. We ask the provider not to store the responses for later retrieval; the provider's own policies on safety monitoring still apply.
- Facebook access tokens are stored encrypted and are never shown in the app.
- Users only see what their role allows. A client user sees only their own business, and only invoices that were sent to them.
- Actions by people and by the AI are written to an activity log that managers and the owner can read.

9. LIMITS AND PROBLEMS
- Daily limits apply to how many AI requests an account can make.
- If a wrong post goes out, tell the operator right away so it can be removed and the cause checked.
- The operator may disable an account to protect a client or the service.
