AI USE AND APPROVAL AGREEMENT
Arisu Office

You must read and accept this before using Arisu Office. If the text changes, you will be asked to accept it again.

1. WHAT ARISU OFFICE DOES
Arisu Office uses AI "employees" to prepare Facebook content for a business: post ideas, post drafts, a quality review of the drafts, and draft replies to public comments. The AI prepares work. People decide.

2. WHAT THE AI MAY DO ON ITS OWN
- Suggest content ideas.
- Write draft posts and draft replies.
- Review drafts and flag problems.
- Suggest a good time to post.
Drafts are only suggestions. They are saved inside Arisu Office and are not visible on Facebook.

3. WHAT THE AI MAY NEVER DO WITHOUT A PERSON'S APPROVAL
- Publish or schedule a post on a Facebook Page.
- Send or post any reply to a comment or message.
- Contact a customer or any other person.
- Spend money, run ads, or change a budget.
- Delete content, or change Page settings or roles.
- State a price, discount, date, guarantee, or testimonial that a person did not provide.
In this version of Arisu Office, replies are never sent by the system at all. A person copies an approved reply and posts it themselves.

4. WHAT THE AI MUST NEVER DO, EVEN WITH APPROVAL
- Pretend to be a human, or pretend a post was written by someone it was not.
- Handle payments or bank details.
- Give medical, legal, or financial advice or guarantees.
- Use one client's information for another client.
- Post engagement bait, or content that breaks Facebook's rules or the law.

5. HOW APPROVAL WORKS
1) The AI writes a draft. 2) Automatic checks and an AI reviewer flag problems. 3) A person reads the exact text and approves or rejects it. 4) Only an approved post can be published. 5) If anyone edits the text after approval, the approval is cancelled and the post must be approved again. Every approval is recorded with the person's name and the time. The approved text is the only text that can be published.
A client may require that only the client's own account can approve their posts.

6. YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES
- AI makes mistakes. It can be wrong, out of date, or tone-deaf. Read every draft before you approve it.
- By approving, you take responsibility for the post.
- Give accurate information about the business. Do not put passwords, ID numbers, health information, or other private personal data into topics, brand notes, or pasted comments.
- Only connect Facebook Pages that you are allowed to manage, and only post content you have the right to use.

7. YOUR DATA
- What is sent to the AI provider (OpenAI) to do the work: the brand notes, the topic you type, and any comment you paste in. We ask the provider not to store the responses for later retrieval; the provider's own policies on safety monitoring still apply.
- Facebook access tokens are stored encrypted and are never shown in the app.
- Users only see what their role allows. A client user sees only their own business.
- Actions by people and by the AI are written to an activity log that managers and the owner can read.

8. LIMITS AND PROBLEMS
- Daily limits apply to how many AI requests an account can make.
- If a wrong post goes out, tell the operator right away so it can be removed and the cause checked.
- The operator may disable an account to protect a client or the service.

By ticking the box and pressing Accept, you confirm you understand that the AI only prepares drafts and that nothing is published until a person approves it.
